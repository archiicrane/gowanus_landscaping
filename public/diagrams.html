Spatial analysis drawn from mapped GeoJSON layers: street trees, buildings, flood vulnerability, stormwater outfalls, and proposed planting data. The central question: how does Gowanus move from ~4% existing canopy cover to the NYC Urban Forest Agenda target of 30%?

Ecological Performance

Canopy Coverage: 4% → 30%

Gowanus holds ~4% canopy coverage from mapped street trees — against NYC's 30% Urban Forest Agenda target. Closing the gap requires strategic rewilding across open land, bioswale corridors, and contamination-cleared zones. Site area and building footprints computed with Turf.js from the mapped datasets.
